00 / A QUESTION I KEEP COMING BACK TO

What if we just
can’t tell?

Imagine two situations that look exactly the same to an AI system. In one, an update helps. In the other, it makes things worse. How could the system know which situation it is in?

Under the assumptions I study, sometimes it can’t. That’s the impossibility question behind K-Bound, and why I care about systems that can say “I’m not sure” before they act.

01
RESEARCH MANUSCRIPT

K-Bound: When Is Label-Free Adaptation Knowable?

Helpful, harmful, and unknowable regimes under distribution shift.

+

When a model sees unfamiliar data without correct labels, how can it know whether an update will help? K-Bound studies when the available evidence supports adapting, freezing, or abstaining. A separate practical method, KGA, uses calibrated benefit intervals learned from earlier labeled cases.

The work separates a theoretical decision frontier from empirical advice. Guarantees depend on explicit assumptions and coverage; the project does not claim that adaptation always helps.

02
RESEARCH MONOGRAPH

ORIUS: Observation–Reality Integrity for Universal Safety

Runtime safety when the measurements cannot be taken at face value.

+

A controller can look safe according to its sensors while the physical system is already outside its constraints. ORIUS connects observation quality, uncertainty, action repair, fallback behavior, and auditable certificates at the moment an action is released.

Battery dispatch is the primary reference implementation. Autonomous-vehicle replay and retrospective healthcare monitoring provide additional, bounded application studies. The depth of evidence differs across domains.

03
TECHNICAL REPORT

A Leakage-Audited AutoML Benchmark

Fair comparisons, explainability, and experiments you can reproduce.

+

High benchmark scores are only useful when the comparison is trustworthy. This project audits leakage, fits preprocessing inside training folds, and compares AutoML frameworks with boosting baselines under shared splits, seeds, and time budgets.

The pipeline brings together feature ablations, statistical reporting, SHAP explanations, a Streamlit leaderboard, and a FastAPI deployment surface.

04
RESEARCH DRAFT / AVAILABLE ON REQUEST

ELARA

Reliability-gated multimodal anomaly fusion under drift.

+

Different modalities can become unreliable in different ways. ELARA explores how reliability signals can guide multimodal anomaly fusion when the data distribution changes, rather than treating every input as equally trustworthy.

This is a research draft. Contact me for the manuscript and to discuss the current scope.
